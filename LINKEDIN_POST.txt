🚀 10 DAYS OF AI VOICE AGENTS: Day 10/10 — The Complete Retrospective & Launch! 🎙️🇮🇳

Over the last 10 days of the #VoiceForBharat Challenge 2026, I built an autonomous, privacy-conscious Health Access Voice Assistant designed to make healthcare guidance accessible to every citizen in India in their native language!

Crucially, the entire voice pipeline is powered by Murf Falcon — the fastest Text-to-Speech (TTS) API in the industry — delivering instantaneous, human-like voice synthesis with zero latency.

---

🌟 WHAT WAS BUILT ACROSS THE 10 DAYS:

🔹 Day 1: Voice Pipeline & Setup
Configured LiveKit Agents WebRTC transport with Deepgram Nova-3 multilingual STT, Google Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite LLM, and Murf Falcon TTS (Anisha voice).

🔹 Day 2: Native Script Mirroring & Guardrails
Enforced strict native script rules in system prompts (Hindi in Devanagari script: नमस्ते, Tamil in Tamil script) so Murf Falcon speaks with an authentic, natural Indian accent instead of an English accent.

🔹 Day 3: 3D Dark Orb WebGL UI & 5 Agent States
Built an interactive 3D WebGL Dark Orb particle visualizer displaying 5 dynamic Agent States: Ready, Connecting, Listening, Speaking, and Ended.

🔹 Day 4: Persistent Memory with Consent Protocol
Integrated an SQLite database (user_memory table) that remembers caller preferences, past symptoms, age, and home district across calls — strictly obeying a Consent-First Protocol.

🔹 Day 5: Live Domain Tools & Graceful Offline Fallbacks
Built three core data tools:
- find_nearby_health_centre: Live OpenStreetMap Overpass lookup for Primary Health Centres (PHCs).
- check_local_health_advisory: Live Open-Meteo temperature, heat index, and AQI readings.
- assess_symptom_urgency: Local deterministic triage sorting symptoms into Red (emergency), Amber (see doctor), or Green (self-care).
(If live APIs time out in rural areas, the agent gracefully admits failure out loud and uses an offline hospital list!)

🔹 Day 6: Outbound Calls & Reminders
Added tool_trigger_outbound_reminder for scheduling medication reminders and follow-up patient check-ins.

🔹 Day 7: Emergency Escalation (ASHA Workers / Doctors)
Red-flag symptoms (chest pain, stroke signs, breathlessness) trigger immediate 112 emergency warnings and create an escalation summary in SQLite for human ASHA workers/doctors.

🔹 Day 8: Call Analytics Dashboard
Built a live Call Analytics Dashboard (/analytics) powered by SQLite (call_analytics table) tracking Total Calls, Success %, Failed Calls, and Escalations.

🔹 Day 9: Specialist Agent Handoff
Created a Clinic & Appointment Specialist (ClinicAppointmentAgent) powered by Murf Falcon (voice: Pooja). When callers ask to book a slot, the Main Agent (voice: Anisha) seamlessly transfers the caller's context to Pooja with a distinct voice switch and UI badge!

🔹 Day 10: Technical Retrospective & Open Source Release!
